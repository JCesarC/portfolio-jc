## Context

Chiplo helps real-estate agents and brokers originate mortgages. Before a file reaches a lender, an advisor has to estimate whether the applicant can afford the payment based on their bank statements.

<Todo>How long did a manual review take, and how many applications per month went through this step?</Todo>

## What I built

A pipeline that reads the applicant's uploaded bank statements and produces a payment-capacity estimate with a written justification an advisor can check:

<Flow
  steps={[
    'Bank statements uploaded',
    'Income categorized (GPT-4)',
    'Credit policy by employment type',
    'Anomalous deposits excluded',
    'Capacity vs. 40% DTI',
    'Reasoning shown in Next.js',
  ]}
/>

- **Income categorization.** GPT-4 categorizes the income found in each uploaded statement.
- **Credit policy by employment type.** Different rules apply depending on how the applicant earns their income.
- **Anomaly exclusion.** Anomalous deposits are excluded so they do not inflate the estimate.
- **Explainability.** Every estimate ships with a reasoning summary in the Next.js app, so advisors can see _why_ and override when needed.
